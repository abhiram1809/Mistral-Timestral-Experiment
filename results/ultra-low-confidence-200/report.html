The confidence estimates are unchanged

Confidence is the saved estimated probability of positive next-bar P&L after fees, financing and short borrow at each proposed exposure. Model quantiles give a break-even probability; a monotone sigmoid fitted on 300 earlier forecast origins calibrates it separately for each instrument/timeframe. The forecast direction, probabilities and calibration remain frozen.

Only the admission gates changed. A 0.1% gate means 0.001 probability, not 10%. The highest passing level wins: 1× needs 0.1%, 2× needs 1%, and 3× needs 5% under the primary schedule. For example, saved probabilities near 0.33% pass the 1× gate but fail the higher gates.

Lower gates accept trades with lower estimated chances of profit. They do not improve the estimates or their accuracy. Mean confidence and realized net win rates are shown below, including minute cases.

Unchanged simulation assumptions

  • One market per category: Reliance (India), SPY (U.S.) and EUR/USD (FX); 200 opportunities each on minute, hourly and daily bars.
  • Identical Yahoo prices, 250-close model contexts, directions, frozen calibration and holding periods as the earlier report.
  • Ideal current-close to next-observed-close fills; each selected trade pays a new entry and exit cost. Same-close execution excludes latency.
  • Per-fill fees: Reliance 10 basis points, SPY 5, EUR/USD 2. Annual financing: 8% on actual exposure above 1×; short borrow: 3%. Elapsed calendar time includes weekends.
  • Effective exposure is nominal leverage / (1 + per-fill fee × nominal leverage), reserving the entry fee. Cash earns no interest.
  • No FX carry, taxes or intrabar margin liquidation. Daily prices are retrospectively adjusted; model training overlap is unknown. No inference rerun, data download, calibration refit or real order was needed.
