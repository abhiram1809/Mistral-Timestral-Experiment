100 trades. Three markets.
Minute, hourly and daily data.

One instrument per category: Reliance for India, SPY for the U.S., and EUR/USD for currencies. Nine cases, exactly 100 completed trades each. Every account starts at 100,000 in its quote currency.

Feed the model the last 250 closing prices. If its next-close median is higher, go long; if lower, go short. Hold until the next closing quote and close the trade. Position size stays at 1×. Exact no-change predictions are skipped until 100 actual trades are collected.

No confidence calibration, trade filter, volatility sizing or discretionary signal changes are used.

“Before costs” is the raw price simulation. “After costs” charges both entry and exit: 0.10% per fill for Reliance, 0.05% for SPY, and 0.02% for EUR/USD. Profit is compounded between trades. These are cost assumptions; funding, borrow fees and FX carry are excluded.

Simulation assumptions

This is a model-only paper simulation with ideal fills at the last observed closing quote immediately after the forecast. It does not model inference or order latency. Yahoo's EUR/USD minute OHLC bars are point quotes, so consecutive closes are used consistently for all cases.

The bar size changes the elapsed time covered by 100 trades. Yahoo hourly equity data can include a shorter final bar at the session close. No missing prices are filled. Daily stock closes are adjusted for corporate actions. The released 209M checkpoint is used with its default forecast settings and no fine-tuning; historical training overlap remains unknown.
