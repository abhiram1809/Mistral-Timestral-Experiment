Unchanged rules and costs

  • Confidence is estimated probability of positive next-bar P&L after fees, financing and borrow. The median forecast chooses direction.
  • Use 3× at its own confidence ≥70%, otherwise 2× at ≥60%, otherwise 1× at ≥55%; otherwise skip. Flat forecasts always skip.
  • Official pinned Timestral-209M, 250-close context, 300 earlier calibration origins, one-bar embargo and frozen calibration maps.
  • Ideal observed-close to next-close fills. Fees per fill: Reliance 10 basis points, SPY 5, EUR/USD 2; annual financing 8% above 1× and short borrow 3%.
  • Separate 100,000 accounts in INR for Reliance and USD for SPY and EUR/USD. No pooled currency P&L, taxes, FX carry or intrabar liquidation.
  • No resampling, duplicate trades, lower gates, calibration refits or live orders. Historical training overlap remains unknown.
